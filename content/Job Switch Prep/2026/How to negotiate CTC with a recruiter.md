# How to negotiate CTC with a recruiter
Candidate must have confidence to negotiate.
- you can ask them the maximum budget
- study about organization
- you can negotiate with the skills you can bring into the table.

research
- how much they can pay
- how much the market can pay


How we can make sure we are not low balled:
- we should have a figure in mind
- build the confidence that you are worth that figure
- try to have a personal rapport with the recruiter.
- try to get the confidence with every conversation and try to correlate with your values.
- ==Why i want this offer?== try to come up with answer with the data points you have.

# 4 major points you can have expectations :
- salary
- perks and benifits
- working mode
- Travel

# Make your employer jealous of you
- stress out the points you can bring into the table
- create an aura that you can fit into the culture(==do your research about it== for specific number)
- If you have other good offers, make sure you show off for next offer.
	- ==Make sure what you are looking for in terms of figures and perks, so that you can save a lot of time not trying to negotiate with the company which cant offer you what you want==
- Try to get involved in every process so you can engage in better negotiations in every chance you get
- evolve in the interview process, with every single round
- dont underestimate yourself and quote a small number


# how to know what recruiters will be looking?
- glassdoor and similar sites and ambitionbox
- your connections
- try to get the numbers/salary range to put your expectations at proper level


# How to realize your worth?
- research with your peer group
- research with friends
