# Use tools to support your habits

## email management
never use your email app as todolist (because you'll be always distracted)

Have dedicated categorization folders in your inbox.

you can also have folders named with Days of week.
you can just drag your mails into those boxes instead of rescheduling.

You can also move the mail to your task management  of choice.

## calendar
he suggests to create a new calendar **Daily Theme calendar**

Also Monthly theme and Weekly theme calendars.
add a all day task. so that you'll be constantly be aware of what is your main theme of the day, week, month.

in todoist , you can use projects, labels, and filters
