## Pain-Pleasure Balance Controls Addiction
[00:29:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3JLaF_4Tz8&t=1750s) 

Same part of the brain who manages pleasure also balances pain.
And these two vary  inversely.

"Wanting more" is we subcosnciously avoiding pain for not having something.

While browsing social media, we mindlessly scroll with a fear of missing out. Whereas it is our brain trying to keep you from experiencing pain of missing out. But There should be a balance. Both Pleasure and Pain should be in balance for any activity.
If we just keep getting pleasure for one activity, who will do other activities.
You cant mindlessly watch youtube videos all day, you got a life to deal with.
Balancing this between activities can make the pleasure much rewarding when you come back next time for the same activity.



## Dopamine Deficits, Anhedonia
[00:30:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3JLaF_4Tz8&t=1847s) 
Once the dopamine hits high, after some time, there'll be decrease in baseline dopamine than its previous normal baseline level.
If we wait, this baseline will be restored to the normal levels as time passes.
But if we continue to indulge ourselves in that compulsive behavior during this dopamine deficit state, it counteracts and decreases the baseline of dopamine much further down the lane.

## [00:55:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3JLaF_4Tz8&t=3325s) Relapse, Craving & Triggers 
For some people they get so used to the substance. They'll end up doing it involuntarily.

here taking an example of an Itch, we might consiously put effort not to scratch an itch on the body. But when we shift our attention/resting/sleeping , we'll scratch that itch intuitively. This is because we are so used to it, we feel its a normal behavior.

### WHy do people relapse?
most of time people relapse even when everything is going well.
Even smaller cues trigger dopamine imbalance.
For those who are struggling with addiction, the trigger can be obvious.

but for some, the trigger can be any success event.
Lets say you are having a pleasurable event and you get a cue that you can enhance this pleasure.
You'll for sure go ahead and do it because your pleasure-pain balance is shifted towards the pain rather than pleasure (even though you are having a pleasure event) and you'll be confused why am i not feeling good even though this event is success.
To justify / enhance the pleasure, You'll expect other pleasure elements adding upon this pleasure experience.
This acts as a cue and you'll go ahead and do it.
