using one's brain to focus on the peripersonal space of the visual system and gradually stepping to the extra personal space and then coming back into the peripersonal space.

This'll train you for the flexibility and control over the ability in our daily life.

Preferable we can do it outside where we can see the horizon (any far object)

>Dont worry about outdoor implemenattion, your brain can come with its own indoor version of anything.

Steps:
1. CLose your eyes, focus on internal processes of the body for a duration of 3 slow breaths (its up to you how much time)
2. open your eyes, focus on the surface of your body.
	1. example would be focusing on the palm of your hand.
	2. ratio of split of attention is 90:10 -> 90% of attention is internal and 10% is external (hand)
3. move 90% of your attention to something far in your room while keeping 10% attention on your breath.
4. try to move 99-100% of your attention some far object in horizone for a particular amount of time.
5. Expand your visual landscape as broadly as you can
6. Return immediately to internal landscape (step 1)

do it for 2-3 times at a time.

This will help us orient our brain in different locations in space and different locations in time.

This'll make the goal setting and achieving very easier for the brain.

---
This is important because goals have different stages in different timeframes.

and training our brain to be flexible across these stages of the goal persuit can enhance its ability to carry out the process.
