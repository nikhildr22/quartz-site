==Peak to BaseLine ratio==
>The amout of motivation/pleasure you experience is always relative to the baseline amount of dopamine in your system.
>With a high baseline, even the peak seems like little.
>Maintaining a good peak to baseline ratio can make wonders in how you feel for every action.

There mainly two ways how neurons communicate with one another:
- fast electrical synopses(sodium ions)
- G Protein coupled receptors (slow)
	- its a chained action, it is slow but long lasting as it is transferred by slow exchange by coupling.


How much interesting a thing to you at this right movement is always relative to the last peak of dopamine you experienced.
For example, when scrolling a social feed, when you come across a very interesting/pleasuring post, the next interesting post may seem very less interesting as the previous post had spiked up your dopamine so high, and as the dopamine is slow to detoriate from the system, your baseline will be high at this movement. So this post may not seem that pleasurable. So you keep on scrolling to get the higher spike, Hoping to find a better post that can hopefully spike up your dopamine again.
