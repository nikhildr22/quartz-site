# Nostalgia for the Future
___
References:

Links: [[Fleeting Notes]] , [[Nostalgia for the Future Application]]


Created: 25-April-2022 Monday || 06:14 AM
_Look forward, not backward. Develop what one of the automobile manufacturers calls “the forward look.” Develop a “nostalgia for the future” instead of for the past. The “forward look” and a “nostalgia for the future” can keep you youthful._
