# Compassion
[[Compassion towards Others leads to Self-Compassion]]
_One of the best known methods of getting over a feeling of guilt is to stop condemning other people in your own mind—stop judging them—stop blaming them and hating them for their mistakes. You will develop a better and more adequate self-image when you begin to feel that other people are more worthy._

This is true, because "guilt" purely originates from our opinions on other people's opinions. If we change our way of thinking - accepting other people's actions,and behaviors, we'll automatically pardon ourself of our so called guilt, because you dont have to justify that to yourself.
