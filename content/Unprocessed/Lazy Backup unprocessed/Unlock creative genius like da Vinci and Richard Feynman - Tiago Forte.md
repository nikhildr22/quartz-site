# did history's great minds Keep 'commonplace books'?
we cant be the first ones with information overload, whenever there was a lot of changes happening around us, they needed a commonplace book.

it'll be **central repository** for all things going around you.

# How do we determine what is important to capture?
1. capture something that makes you feeling/inspiring.
2. Sometimes you need to store some nerdy information that you can use in the future.
3. Some wisdom and life lessons that only makes sense to you.
4. Information that are novel and surpises you.

its your responsibility is to find the signal in the noise.
the signal you pick might be very specific to you, and completely useless to others.

## having a few favourite questions
with  these favourite questions in mind, whenever you come across a novel thing, you can think of how can this be used to solve your favourite question.
You can capture your insights based on these favourite questions.

# What are the benefits and limitations of capturing?
less stress, good health, peace of mind.

>By writing down things you are letting go of the pressure of witholding that critical info or fear of loosing it. This lets us relax a bit and focus on the things at hand.

When there are a lot of changes and lot of new information coming in, you can use knowledge capture to ease the process.

# What are divergence and convergence?
first we'll use divergence, to explore all possibilities.
then we'll use convergence.
we need divergence because to find out already existing solutions and you dont reinvent the wheel.
when you are in the divergent phase, dont be afraid of the rabit holes. talk to people, walk in the street, explore any possible information .

At some point, you need to start converging, eliminating the distractions.

This has to be done back and forth until you come up with a working solution.

## Adding constraints for divergence
due to infinite number of solutions out there, there are endless possible outcomes you can explore.
but to get the work done, we need to add constraints.
example: if you decide what to eat for dinner, the possibilities are endless.
but if you already have a list of 3-4 items, its easy to choose and you are done with that decision.

==give yourself constraints for divergent process.
Add time, constraint of choices etc==

## How'd you converge?
- break down things into smaller pieces.
- write down next thing todo before wrapping up current work.
- focusing on important things and postponing less important things to the next iteration of working session/release -> this was followed in SDE process where we reduce the scope of the release, and deliver the important features and keep the less important features for the next release.

# When do we pivot between divergence and convergence?

when you get an internal confidence that you've enough information , you can start to converge.
