# TCS



Tata Consultancy Services Limited (TCS) is a
subsidiary of the Tata Group, a largest Indian
information technology company that provides
global business consulting and information
technology services. TCS helps clients in 50+
countries to create and execute different
strategies for their digital transformation.

======

TCS Limited was founded in 1968 by a division
of Tata Sons Limited. TCS also established India's
first software Centre.
On 25 August 2004, TCS became a Publicly
Listed Company.
Tagline Of The Company :
Experience Certainty


======
**Rajesh Gopinathan** is the Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), a leading global IT solutions and consulting firm. He was elevated to the role of Chief Executive Officer in February 2017 after serving as the Chief Financial Officer since 2013.

=====
TCS Product Service
TCS provides a wide range of information technology-
related products and services including application
development, business process outsourcing, capacity
planning, consulting, enterprise software, hardware
sizing, payment processing, software management,
and technology education services. TCS established
software products are TCS BaNCS and TCS
MasterCraft.
Education Platform : TCS ION
