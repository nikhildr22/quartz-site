# 3 step approach to deal with stress  
  
1. Decouple stress  
	1. understand it's neutral / or yet to be determined effect of experiencing or anticipating adversity in your goal related efforts.  
	2. Adversity or challenge in something that is working against you.  
	3. Adversity in your goal related efforts.  
3rd point Is very important. We stress about things we care.  
2. welcome it  
	1. What is it I'm trying to do with this stress and how I'm feeling about it  
3. use the stress response to achieve the thing we care about, not spending the time, money and efforts to get rid of the stress
