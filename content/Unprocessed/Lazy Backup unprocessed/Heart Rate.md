# Heart Rate

# Resting Heart rate
heart rate at rest
it can be measured first thing in the morning.
or throughout the night.

60 to 100 is normal.

if resting heart rate increases without any reason, to check if you are feeling well.
reasons for change in resting heart rate:
- lack of sleep
- over-training
- stress

# Heart rate recovery
after an exercise, the time it takes to heart rate to come down back to normal.

for a normal healthy human the faster it comes back, the better.
