# Control
Organize, make sense of, and curtail obligations in both your professional and personal life.

• **Create breathing room and space to think**.

• **Allow for enjoyment** of what is good in your life.

• **Provide freedom from anxiety caused by overload and disorganization**.

• **Grant freedom to experiment with new things** and add important elements to your life

1. **Multi-Scale Planning (for professional life)**:

    ◦ This system involves planning at three different time scales: **seasonal/quarterly, weekly, and daily**.

    ◦ You create a quarterly or semester plan, update it regularly, and then use it to inform a weekly plan. The weekly plan, in turn, informs a daily time-block plan.

    ◦ This puts you in control of your professional time, providing clear feedback on your workload, how long tasks take, and what is most burdensome

2. **Household Planning (for life outside of work)**:

    ◦ This involves establishing a basic system for non-work-related tasks and obligations, such as chores, errands, and personal appointments.

    ◦ It typically includes a **full capture system** where all non-professional tasks are written down

3. **Automate and Curtail**:

    ◦ The goal of implementing these organizational systems is not to fit more work into your life, but to gain information and control to **make your life better and less busy**
Automate

• **What it means**: This involves finding ways to make **recurring tasks** happen regularly without needing to constantly remember and schedule them on an ad-hoc basis. The goal is to reduce the "planning footprint" of these tasks, freeing up your cognitive energy

Curtail

• **What it means**: This is about **strategically getting rid of or significantly reducing obligations** that are overwhelming you, provide little return, or are simply not serving your goals.


# Vision
The primary purpose of the vision layer is to **take specific aspects of your life and overhaul them to be more remarkable**. It's where you figure out **"where to aim" your energies** now that you have built a strong foundation from the preceding layers

**Key Actions: One Small Plan, One Large**

1. One Small Overhaul

This involves taking **one specific area of your life, preferably a non-professional one, and conducting a full overhaul to make it more remarkable**

2. One Larger Overhaul

This action focuses on **beginning the process of overhauling a larger area of your life**, such as your career or where you live. Unlike the small overhaul, this is typically a **longer-term project that could take a year or two to fully implement**

It requires you to:

• **Envision your ideal lifestyle five or ten years from now**. This vision should capture all aspects of your life and be visceral—what does it feel like, what do you see, what do you hear, what's the rhythm of your day-to-day life, and what resonates with you from external sources like magazines or documentaries?

• **"Scheme" the steps involved** to move your life in that desired direction. This might include building new skills, pursuing specific promotions, or growing a side business to a certain income level before making a major career change.
