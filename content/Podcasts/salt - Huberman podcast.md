- Osmotic thirst
- Hypovolemic thirst

amount of salt in your body will affect every other organ as they are made of cells and cells indeed contain water.
And water follows the salt (osmosis).
This is the case for brain too.
If there is more salt concentration in the brain cells, the water rushes in and the brain cells swell.
if the salt concentration is low, the water rushes out of the cells.

There are lot of studies done studying is it better to have more salt inside the cell or less salt.

We should note that majority of studies point that if there overconsumption of salt is bad for brain and longetivity.
if overconsumed, we have more salt in the system when compared to the cells, the cells will shrink due to osmotic pressure.

But also there are some decent studies that state if the salt consumtion is too low, it is harmful for brain health and longetivity.

>Its very complicated to conduct these studies in a controlled way as High/low level of salt in food come with different set of foods, which indeed can affect the study. (high/low carbohydrates/fats etc)
