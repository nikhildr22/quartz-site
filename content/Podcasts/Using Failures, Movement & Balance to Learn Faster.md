Age factor for plasticity:

if you are less than 25, brain is already conditioned for neuroplasticity.
Suggestion will be to try a broad range of things and stick to the one that you really like and you are really good  at, rather than entering the rat race.

If you have passed this phase,
you can tap into these plasticity mechanisms by triggering few chemical signals in your brain.


How to trigger?

we have a map of perception of our world.
maps of visual space, maps of auditory space and maps of motor space are aligned together in brain.
for example:
we have specific neuron to idenify the sound from a specific direction.
lets say you heard a sound from 15 degree to your front.
you'll look that way because, you have specific neurons to identify 15 degree space in visual cortel.
and same thing with your motor space.

==Trick is that, these maps are plastic==

In one experiment, they tried modifying visual space using glass or prism.
People started adjusting their motor behavior by correcting the other neurons to sync.
the layer of maps can shift, rotate and even flip.

This proved that error signals triggered the plasticity.

Initially, when there are a lot of errors, you feel frustrated.
brain does not feel frustrated, it just experiences a set of chemicals.

When a error is made, brain releases a set of chemicals trying to balace the error.
everytime an error is made, this process is repeated. And when a successful move is made. Dopamine is release in higher amount than other chemicals making the neural pathway rewarding for the successful action. ^827804

>Trigger frustration and try to stay in that frustration trying new things (insanity).
>Do not walk away when you feel frustrated.
>This'll trigger neuroplasticity.

This is very easy for young people, in adults, its comparitively hard.
To make it tolerable, incremental learning with small errors are very efficient when you are adult.

>==Having a reward for something, will increase the success rate.==
