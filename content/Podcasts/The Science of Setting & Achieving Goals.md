## Dopamine Reward Prediction Error, Controlling Dopamine 
[01:34:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t1F7EEGPQwo&t=5666s) 
Dopamine release is governed by Reward prediction error.

Dopamine release is high for novelty.

i.e., when you dont expect the pleasure, but it appears of nowhere. It leads to high hits of dopamine.

We can leverage this for our goal persuits by setting the right milestones for the goal.

>When playing a game, you'll get a good amount of pleasure when you score very good score without any expectations.
>This'll lead to high dose of dopamine.
>As this event was unexpected, you'll hook on to the game, expecting that novelty again and again.
>This goes same for mindless browsing as well.
